Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Technocon EOOD, registered in Bulgaria (ul. Osogovo 51, 1303 Sofia). Contact: office@techno-con.eu.

2. What data we collect and why

We collect data only for the following purposes:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4) — only if you accept cookies. We collect anonymised usage data (pages visited, session duration, device type, approximate location at country/city level). IP addresses are anonymised before storage. This helps us understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • Contact form — when you submit the contact form, we receive your name, company, email address and message. This data is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not shared with third parties.
  • Cookie consent preference — we store a cookie (tc_consent) recording your accept/decline choice. This cookie contains no personal data.

3. Cookies

We use two categories of cookies:

  • Essential — tc_consent: stores your cookie preference. Valid for 1 year. No personal data.
  • Analytics (optional) — Google Analytics 4 cookies (_ga, _ga_*): set only after you accept. Used to measure site traffic anonymously. Valid for up to 2 years. See Google's Privacy Policy.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or clicking "Decline" when the banner reappears.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Analytics cookies: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
  • Contact form: Legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with any third party except:

  • Google LLC — analytics data under Google's data processing agreement, with IP anonymisation enabled. Google may transfer data to the US under Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Data retention

  • Contact form enquiries: retained for up to 3 years for business correspondence purposes.
  • Analytics data: retained for 14 months in Google Analytics, then automatically deleted.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to: access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data; withdraw consent at any time; lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg).

To exercise any right, email office@techno-con.eu.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Contact form submissions are transmitted encrypted. We do not store payment data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
